- Maximum occupancy: 5 guests.
- Fifth guest: LKR 800.

9. DOCUMENT SAFETY, PRIVACY, AND OPEN DECISIONS

Preserve and extend the existing safety requirements:

- Emergency egress cannot depend on cloud access, payment,
  PIN entry, or an administrator.

- Access revocation must not trap occupants.
  Remote power actions must not disable safety systems.

- Unknown/offline is not OFF, available, or unoccupied.

- Remote commands distinguish requested, pending, acknowledged,
  failed, expired, and unknown states.

- Offline capabilities depend on tested lock/controller compatibility.
  Cloud remote unlock is not an offline guarantee.

- OCR text extraction is not proof of document authenticity,
  identity, or liveness.

- Manual review, least-privilege evidence access, private encrypted
  storage, retention decisions, and deletion workflows are
  required design topics.

- Google login is not a substitute for explicit, versioned
  policy acceptance.

Track open decisions for:

- Final stack, cloud, and providers.
- Hardware and offline behavior.
- All-guest verification.
- Cleaning and emergency staffing.
- Privacy and retention.
- Commercial screening and gaming rights.
- Refunds and card re-billing.
- Occupancy detection.
- Loyalty rules.
- Hold duration.
- Closing-time cleaning.
